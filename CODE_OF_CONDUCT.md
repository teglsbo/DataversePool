# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
v2.1, summarized here:

- Be respectful, welcoming, and constructive in all project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions).
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language/behavior.
- Assume good faith; disagree on technical merits, not on people.
- Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributors that violate this code, and may report
  serious or repeated violations as appropriate.

To report a concern, open a private security advisory or contact the maintainers listed in
`SECURITY.md`. Reports are handled confidentially.
