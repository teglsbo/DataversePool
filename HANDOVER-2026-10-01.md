## Answer to the original question (telemetry)

- **`x-ms-ratelimit-*` headers on successful calls:** not reachable through `ServiceClient`. The
  strings are absent from the SDK. ADR-0008's conclusion holds.
- **`x-ms-dop-hint`:** reachable. `ServiceClient.RecommendedDegreesOfParallelism` is a public
  property fed by that header on both transports. ADR-0006 and ADR-0016 wrongly say it is out of
  reach. The library never reads it.
- **Counting frontend servers:** there is no designed signal. The only per-response server identity
  is the `ARRAffinity` cookie value, which gives an approximation. It needs response capture
  (`DiagnosticListener` on `HttpHandlerDiagnosticListener` is the least invasive route).
